[Full description]
Serve freshly fried tempura to the customers at your counter in this 3D cooking game.

HOW TO PLAY
- Customers order with a picture in a speech bubble: shrimp, whiting, eel, scallop, oyster, eggplant, shishito pepper, lotus root, maitake and kakiage
- Pick an ingredient: it is dipped in batter and dropped into the copper pot
- Only the side in the oil browns, so tap to flip it halfway
- When it is done, drag it to the rack, let the oil drain, and it is served

READ THE BUBBLES AND THE SOUND
Right after it goes in, big bubbles burst and steam rises. As the moisture cooks off, the bubbles get small and the sizzle turns into a light crackle. Golden brown scores high; too early and it is raw inside, too late and it burns. Put too many pieces in at once and the oil cools down, making them soggy.

EARN YOUR RANK
Each 2:30 shift gives you your sales and a rank, from Apprentice to Master and Tempura God. A frying-time guide appears for the first piece of each ingredient, so beginners can learn the timing. Once you know it, try Master mode, which hides the seconds for a x1.3 score.

DETAILS
- A hammered copper pot, clear oil, crunchy batter and a cypress counter in 3D
- Realistic sizzling and crackling sounds
- Music: J.S. Bach, Prelude in C major
- Japanese and English. Playable offline
